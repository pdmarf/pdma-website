ExpensePlus Migration Support

from £880 per migration

(excluding VAT)

Moving to a new accounting system can feel like a big step, especially when it comes to charity finances. If you're currently managing your accounts on spreadsheets, another accounting package, or an older system, migrating to the excellent ExpensePlus fund accounting software is one of the best things you can do to save time, money, and stress in your bookkeeping.

ExpensePlus is specifically designed for charities and nonprofit organisations and, in our opinion, it's the best system you could choose for charity bookkeeping. That's why we offer a dedicated ExpensePlus Migration Support service, to help make your transition as smooth, efficient and stress-free as possible.

If your charity has already moved on to ExpensePlus but you'd like your setup reviewed in depth by us, we can apply a modified migration service to review your initial setup.

Our day rates for the package below are based on income size, to make things as accessible as possible for charities of all shapes and sizes.

Our Migration Service includes:

  • A review of your current accounting structures and practices
  • The design of a new fund accounting architecture for use inside ExpensePlus
  • The setup of ExpensePlus, including User Accounts, Opening Balances, Bank Accounts, Funds, Budget Categories, and Invoice Templates
  • We'll oversee 'going live' on ExpensePlus, and will monitor and adjust the setup as necessary
  • The provision of basic ExpensePlus training for internal stakeholders (Basic Users, Finance Assistants, Budget Holders & Trustees) in the form of high quality, on-demand video training
  • A comprehensive handover with your bookkeeper during a 2-hour training package via video call
  • 1 month of priority email support for your team if they have any further questions

Once you're up and running on ExpensePlus, you can also add our BookkeepingPlus Service (optional) to outsource your ongoing bookkeeping and free up your team.
